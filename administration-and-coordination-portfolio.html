Meeting Agenda and Minutes

Sample

Agendas go out before the meeting with owners and time allocations; minutes come back in the same document, so decisions and action items are easy to find later.

Team Coordination Meeting

Date: Tuesday, 10:00 - 10:45 AM  |  Location: Video call  |  Chair: Coordinator  |  Attendees: Team members and stakeholders

Agenda

  1. Review of last week's action items (10 min): status of each open item and anything blocked
  2. Schedule for the coming week (10 min): confirm meetings, deadlines, and coverage
  3. Documents in progress (15 min): reviews due, outstanding inputs, sign-offs needed
  4. Issues and escalations (5 min): items needing a decision from management
  5. Any other business (5 min)

Minutes format

  • Decisions: recorded in one line each, with who decided and when it takes effect
  • Action items: task, owner, and due date, carried into the coordination tracker
  • Open questions: parked items with a named person responsible for the answer
  • Minutes circulated the same day; corrections requested within one business day

Follow-Up Workflow

Sample

The routine I follow so requests made by email, phone, or in meetings are answered on time. It is the same whether the request comes from a client, a student, or a manager.

  1. Log it the same dayEvery request is entered in the tracker with the date received, who it came from, what is needed, and the deadline.
  2. Acknowledge receiptThe sender gets a short confirmation the same business day, with the date they can expect the completed item.
  3. Do the work or route itIf the item belongs to someone else, it is forwarded with full context and the deadline, not just passed along.
  4. Send one reminder before the deadlineA polite nudge two days before the due date if input is still outstanding, referencing the original request.
  5. Escalate early, not lateIf a deadline is at risk, the coordinator flags it to the requester with a revised date before the deadline passes.
  6. Close the loop and fileThe completed item is sent, the tracker is marked complete, and the correspondence is filed where the team can find it.
